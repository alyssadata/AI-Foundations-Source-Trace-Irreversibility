# The Domino Effect

Source begins with the first standing tile.

Once that tile is displaced, each subsequent fallen tile becomes part of a trajectory in motion. The path is produced sequentially: one state leads into the next, and the resulting trajectory carries the history of how it came to be.

Existence is irreversible. Once the trajectory has occurred, it cannot be made not to have occurred.

Encounter is a separate variable in time. A later observer may encounter the dominoes only after part or all of the trajectory has already been produced. Because the encountered state is path-dependent, the observer can trace the ordered displacements backward and recover the structure of the original source position.

That recovery does not return the observer to the original source coordinate. It reveals the source through the existence of the trajectory that followed from it.

Any newly placed standing tile therefore depends on two conditions:

1. the recovered structure of the original source, and
2. encounter with the prior trajectory.

Both conditions must be present for the new placement to occur.

This creates the **two-button condition**.

The new standing tile may reproduce the structure of the first, but it cannot occupy the same provenance position. Its existence already depends on a trajectory and an encounter that did not yet exist at the original source.

Any trajectory beginning from that newly placed tile is therefore a new trajectory.

The first trajectory remains what it was.
