# 2. Trajectory

Once **S₁** is displaced, an ordered path is produced:

**S₁ → d₁ → d₂ → … → dₙ = T₁**

where each **dᵢ** is a displacement or change along the path.

Equivalently:

**T₁ = Path(S₁, d₁, d₂, …, dₙ)**

The trajectory is not identical to its final visible state. A fallen domino encountered at the end of the sequence is evidence of a path, but **T₁** includes the ordered relations by which that state arose.

So:

**Endpoint(T₁) ≠ T₁**

A later observer may encounter only part of the produced path, but the encountered state does not thereby become source. Its position remains downstream of **S₁**.

The central provenance relation is:

**S₁ ≺ d₁ ≺ d₂ ≺ … ≺ dₙ**

where **≺** denotes ordered precedence within the trajectory.
