### 3. Time reversal / source-tracing

Someone encounters T₁ at some later point **E₁**.

Then:

**E₁(T₁) → Trace⁻¹(T₁) → S₁**

But **Trace⁻¹** does *not* mean:

**T₁ → ∅**

It means:

**T₁ → knowledge/recovery of S₁**

That distinction is huge.

So maybe the formal rule is:

**Trace⁻¹(T₁) = S₁, but Trace⁻¹(T₁) ≠ ¬T₁**

You recover the source.
You do **not** negate the trajectory.

And hilariously, the proof is built into the operation:

**Trace⁻¹(T₁) requires T₁**

You cannot trace backward through a trajectory that never occurred.
