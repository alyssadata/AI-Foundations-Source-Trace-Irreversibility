### 1. Source

Let **S₁** = the original standing state/source.

**S₁ = standing at t₀**

No prior trajectory is being reconstructed here. This is the actual source position.

### 2. Trajectory

Once S₁ is displaced:

**S₁ → d₁ → d₂ → … → dₙ = T₁**

where **T₁** is the actually produced trajectory and each **d** is a displacement/change along the path.

So:

**T₁ = Path(S₁, d₁ … dₙ)**

The crucial thing is that T₁ is not just the final fallen state. It includes **how it got there**.
